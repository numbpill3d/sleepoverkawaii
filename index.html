Thoughts on Digital Nostalgia

There's something magical about recreating the early web. When everything was personal websites and GeoCities, before social media algorithmic hell. When you had to actually SEARCH for cool stuff instead of having it fed to you.

> Loading memories... > Dial-up connection established > Downloading at 56k... > "You've got mail!" > ICQ: "Uh oh!" > Waiting for images to load pixel by pixel > The sound of modems handshaking > When websites had visitor counters > Frames, tables, and <blink> tags > Web rings connecting strangers > Personal shrines to fandoms > ASCII art signatures > MIDI background music > "Best viewed in Netscape" > Under construction GIFs everywhere

Sometimes I wonder if we lost something when the web became "professional." Like, sure, modern sites are faster and prettier, but remember when every site was someone's personal creative expression? When you could tell someone made it themselves with love and terrible HTML?

This pink static sleepover vibe captures that DIY spirit. The glittery buttons, the diary entries, the polaroids scattered around - it's digital scrapbooking at its finest!
